NexHRIS — Manuscript Appendices

Seven appendix pages formatted like last year’s manuscript: ISPSC Tagudin letterhead, page numbers, and the BSIT footer. Every diagram is drawn from the working codebase.

Getting these into your manuscript
  1. Press Ctrl + P, choose Save as PDF, paper Letter, margins None, and tick Background graphics. You get a 7-page PDF that drops straight into the appendix section.
  2. To place a single diagram in Word instead, screenshot just the figure area and paste it under your own appendix heading.
